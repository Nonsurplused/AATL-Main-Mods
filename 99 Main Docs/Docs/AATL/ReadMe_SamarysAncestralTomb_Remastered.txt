Samarys Ancestral Tomb: The Forbidden Archives

By PikachunoTM

Requires: 
Latest AATL_Data (https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/50647)
Latest OAAB_Data (https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/49042)

Description:
A remaster of Samarys Ancestral Tomb Expanded (Original Mod here: https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/45612) that connects the resurrection of Lord Brinne with the events of the Cult of Lorkhan, and starts part of a new search across Morrowind for the Crook of the Ancestors.

Synopsis:
"Shadowy figures have travelled through Seyda Neen, heading to the ancient tomb of Samarys. Uncover their true purpose, and resurrect the ancient and powerful Lord Eramis Brinne."
