A Merchant's Whim


Requires: 
Latest AATL_Data (https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/50647)
Latest OAAB_Data (https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/49042)

Description:
A new merchant in Vivec has had a change of heart, and would like your help with a personal matter of his.

Find him in Vivec, Foreign Quarter, and visit him over several days. Gain his trust, and he will ask you to leave an offering in his stead.

Help him, uncover a family's secrets, and help a ghost find peace.
